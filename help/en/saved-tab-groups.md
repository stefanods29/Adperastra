# Saved groups

Save a set of tabs and reopen its supported positions later.

## How to use

- Right-click a group label and choose **Save group**. Open **Saved groups** in the navigation pane or plus menu to reopen it.
- Use **Update saved group** to replace its saved tab list.
- Use **Export groups…** and **Import groups…** to transfer a small group file. Exporting never replaces an existing file.

## Good to know

- Saved groups contain locations and view positions, not unsaved edits or passwords.
- Missing files have a Locate action. Locked folders must be unlocked again.
- Removing a saved group does not delete its files.

See also: "Tab groups" and "Sessions".
