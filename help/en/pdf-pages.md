# Rearranging PDF pages

Open Page tools. Select thumbnails to rotate or delete pages, drag to reorder, or choose Insert pages or Merge PDFs. Split creates new files for the chosen ranges.

## Good to know

The PDF must keep at least one page. Review page order and Undo mistakes before saving. Unsupported cross-page structures may require refusing a change rather than losing data.
