# Customizing folders

Give folders a local color or icon.

## How to use

Select folders and choose **Customize folder**. Pick a color, icon, emoji or image, then **Apply**. **Reset** removes the app appearance.

## Good to know

Files are not renamed. App records follow verified moves; some locations cannot follow external renames. **Apply to Windows Explorer** is separate and writes desktop.ini only after explicit consent.

See also: "Folder backgrounds" and "Column sets".
