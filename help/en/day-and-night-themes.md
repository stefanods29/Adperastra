# Day and night themes

Choose separate appearances for day and night.

## How to use

1. Open **Settings > Day and night**.
2. Select a **Day theme** and a **Night theme**. Each can be a built-in theme or one of your saved custom themes.
3. Choose **Follow Windows**, **Scheduled times** or **Manual**.
   - **Follow Windows** uses the light or dark setting of Windows.
   - **Scheduled times** uses the two local start times below. Enter different times for the day start and the night start.
   - **Manual** uses the day or night appearance you pick.
4. The title-bar button **Override until the next switch** changes the appearance now. It goes back to the schedule at the next actual switch. It is not saved, so restarting the app returns to the schedule.

## Good to know

- Schedules follow this PC's local clock, including daylight-saving changes and waking from sleep.
- Sunrise and sunset require your location. They are not offered in this version; Adperastra does not guess them from the clock.
- High contrast overrides decorative materials so text stays readable.
- A missing custom theme falls back to the matching built-in light or dark theme, with a warning.
