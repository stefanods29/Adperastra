# Cropping and resizing

Choose **Crop** and a ratio, drag the handles or enter the crop values, then rotate or flip if needed. **Size** changes pixels or percentage; keep proportions to avoid distortion. Enlargement does not invent new detail. Review transparent corners after rotation and export dimensions before saving.

## Crop values

- Crop values are in the picture's own pixels and do not include the outer edge.
- **Free** keeps any rectangle. The fixed ratios keep their shape while you drag.
- **Rotate left**, **Rotate right**, **Flip horizontally** and **Flip vertically** change the view that will be saved.
- An angled rotation can leave transparent corners. Choose a background for formats that cannot keep transparency.

## Size

- The width and height are the final exported pixels.
- **Keep proportions** is on by default.
- **Allow enlargement** is required before the app makes a picture larger. It never invents detail that was not in the source.
