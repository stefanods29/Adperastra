# Viewing PDFs

Read PDFs without leaving the Explorer.

## How to use

- Open a PDF.
- Use **Previous page**, **Next page**, or **Page number** to move around.
- Open **Thumbnails** or **Outline** for navigation.
- Choose **Fit width**, **Fit page**, or a zoom level.
- Select text to copy it, or use Find to search.
- Choose **Print…** and a range of up to 100 pages to open the print window.

## Good to know

- PDF scripts and external resources do not run.
- Some scanned pages have no selectable text; text recognition is added separately.
- Passwords are kept only while the PDF is open.
- Very large or damaged PDFs may need another app.
- Preview mode cannot edit or print.

See also: "Opening files" and "Quick preview".
