# Privacy settings

Choose whether future actions keep information or ask you what to remove.

## How to use

Open Privacy in Settings. Keep **Keep all metadata** for normal preservation. Choose **Ask each time** for a review, or explicitly select information to remove. Location warnings and sharing prompts are separate optional switches.

## Good to know

All removal and warning options start off. Settings never authorize background changes to your files. Unsupported information cannot be silently removed.

See also: "Removing hidden information" and "Background work".
