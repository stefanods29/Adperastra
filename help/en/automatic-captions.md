# Automatic captions and dictation

In the active media player, choose Automatic captions and a language. Preparing captions means the next words are still being processed. Seeking prepares the new position. Choosing a subtitle file replaces the automatic captions until you choose them again. In an editor with Dictate, choose Start recording, allow this recording, speak, then choose Stop recording. Review the recognized words before the editor's Apply action. Cancel recording keeps the document unchanged.

## How to use it

Speech tools is optional and works without the assistant. Automatic captions are off by default; their appearance follows Accessibility settings. Only the active media player prepares captions. Dictation records for at most two minutes, shows a recording indicator, and never inserts words automatically. Switching away cancels the recording. Audio is processed on this computer; no recording is saved automatically. Recognition can be wrong.
