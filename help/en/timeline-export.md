# Exporting a timeline

## How to use

Choose **Export timeline…** and a compatible preset. Review dimensions, frame rate, codecs, metadata, subtitles and a new destination. **Preview sample** checks the captured revision; later edits remain in the project.

Start export and keep browsing while its job shows progress and **Cancel**.

## Good to know

The original media and prior files are kept. Save the project separately to retain editable tracks and effects.

See also: "The timeline" and "App storage".
