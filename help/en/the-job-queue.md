# The job queue

Follow long file tasks while you keep browsing.

## How to use

Open the jobs panel to see progress and any problems. Use **Cancel** to stop pending work. Review completed, skipped and failed files. In Quick Convert, **Retry failed files** retries only files that did not finish.

## Good to know

- Cancelling does not remove completed results.
- A summary lists the actual effects.
- Background preparation and user-started conversion are separate controls.

See also: "Converting files" and "Background work".
