# The address bar

The address bar shows where you are and takes you anywhere else.

## Jumping with the mouse

- Click any part of the path to jump straight there: clicking **Users** in `C:\Users\you\Documents` opens `C:\Users`.
- Click the small chevron next to a part to list the folders inside the level above it, then pick one. This is the fastest way to switch between sibling folders.
- Click the empty space at the end of the bar to turn it into a text box. Type or paste a path and press **Enter**. Press **Escape** to cancel.
- The clock button next to Back shows folders you visited in this tab, so you can jump back several steps at once.

## Typing a path

You can type a full path such as `D:\Projects` or a network share such as `\\server\share`. Typing `This PC` shows your drives.
