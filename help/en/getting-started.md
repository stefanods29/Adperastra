# Getting started

Adperastra is one window for your files. You can browse your files, open them, convert them, and edit them without switching apps.

The Explorer tab (Home) is always there. It is where you browse your folders. Files open in their own tabs, next to it. More viewers arrive in later steps, so some file types may not open inside the app yet.

You do not need internet or an account. Everything works offline on your PC.

Press **F1** at any time to open help. You can change the language and theme in **Settings**.
