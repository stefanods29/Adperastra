# Profiles and portable data

Keep separate local settings and data, or copy them beside a portable app.

## How to use

Open **Settings > Profiles**. Create or rename a profile. Switch profile saves or discards changes, finishes active work and restarts. Make portable copy creates a verified copy in an empty destination.

## Good to know

Original data is kept. Shared engine packs are separate from profile caches. Switching cannot leave unlocked content in another profile. Choose a writable portable folder.

See also: "Portable mode" and "Sessions".
