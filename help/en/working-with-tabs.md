# Working with tabs

Keep several folders and files open while you work.

## How to use tabs

- Use **Open in new tab** for a folder or choose **New folder tab** from the plus menu.
- Right-click a tab to pin, duplicate or close it.
- Use Ctrl+Tab to switch and Ctrl+Shift+T to reopen a closed tab.
- Use Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+8 to go to a tab by its position, and Ctrl+9 to go to the last tab.
- Middle-click a tab to close it.

## Good to know

- The permanent Explorer tab cannot close.
- Folder tabs keep independent views.
- Two views of one edited document share its changes, so closing one view does not throw away the other.
- Up to 200 tabs can be open.

See also: "Sessions" and "Opening files".
