# Editing images

Open an image and choose **Edit**. Choose a tool, inspect its preview and apply the change. **Undo** or **Redo** changes your edit history; **Compare** shows the original without changing it. **Save a copy** keeps the original. **Export** chooses another format and explains metadata or transparency changes. Export keeps metadata by default and lists anything it must remove.

## Good to know

- Editing works on a copy of the picture in memory until you save or export.
- A protected or read-only picture opens without editing controls.
- The original file is never changed by Undo, Compare or a preview.
