# The timeline

Choose **Edit in timeline** from a video, or **New timeline project**. Add video and audio tracks, then drag supported files from Explorer, the shelf or other tabs.

## Good to know

Save the `.adtimeline` project to keep editing instructions. Projects reference source media; keep those files available and use **Relink source** when they move. Ordinary project saving does not render a new video.

See also: "Working with timeline clips".
