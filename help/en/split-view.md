# Split view

Keep two compatible views inside one tab.

## How to use split view

- Choose **Open in split view**, or drag an eligible tab onto another tab's split target.
- Choose **Side by side** or **Above and below**.
- Drag the divider or focus it and use the arrow keys.
- Choose **Swap sides** to exchange positions.
- Press F6 to move the focus between the two sides and the toolbar.
- Turn on **Sync scrolling** when both sides support it.

## Good to know

- Commands and the ribbon follow the active side. **Close this side** keeps the other view open.
- Ctrl+W closes the whole split tab after checking unsaved work.
- Sessions and saved groups keep supported split positions.
- Two views of one edited file share its changes: closing one side does not throw the other one away.
- A split contains two sides. To show three views, open another tab.

See also: "Working with tabs" and "Copying content between tabs".
