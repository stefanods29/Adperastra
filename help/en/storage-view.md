# Storage view

See what reported file sizes and allocated space are using.

## How to use

Choose a drive or folder, follow scan progress and drill into the treemap or Largest items list. Open, move or compress selected items through normal actions.

## Good to know

Incomplete scans show partial totals. Online-only files are counted without downloading. Hard links can have several names but share allocated space. A cached result is marked while refreshing.

See also: "Finding duplicates" and "Folder sizes".
