# The shelf

Collect references from several folders without moving the files.

## How to use

Open **Shelf** with Ctrl+Alt+S. Drop files there or choose **Add to shelf**. Select shelf items and choose copy, move, compress, share, convert, privacy cleaning or opening as a tab group. Each action opens its normal review.

## Good to know

Removing or clearing shelf references does not delete files. The list stays after restarting; moved or missing items may need Refresh. Locked-folder items remain in memory only.

See also: “Batch actions” and “Saved groups”.
