# Background work

Choose when Adperastra prepares previews in the background.

## How to use

Open **Settings > Background work**. Turn on **Only when idle** to wait until you have not used the computer for 30 seconds. Turn on **Only on AC power** to wait for the charger. **Pause all background work** stops preparation until you turn it off.

## Good to know

Opening and copying files still work while preparation is paused. The preview cache has a size limit and can be rebuilt. Background preparation never downloads online-only files.

See also: "Thumbnails and views" and "Changing language and theme".
