# Background work

Choose when Adperastra prepares previews and indexes in the background.

## How to use

Open **Settings > Background work**. Enable only the tasks you want. Turn on **Only when idle** to wait until you have not used the computer for 30 seconds. Turn on **Only on AC power** to wait for the charger. Choose any full-screen, quiet-hour and CPU rules you need. **Pause all background work** stops preparation until you turn it off; **Resume** starts eligible tasks again.

## Good to know

Opening and copying files still work while preparation is paused. Jobs you explicitly start keep their own progress and Cancel controls. The preview cache has a size limit and can be rebuilt. Background preparation never downloads online-only files, and these rules do not authorize downloads or file changes.
