# Adjustments and filters

Choose **Adjust** to change brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, sharpness or blur. **Filters** apply a named preset; **Original** resets adjustments while keeping your crop and marks. **Compare** checks the untouched source. Changes remain undoable until their history limit is reached.

## Adjustment values

- Brightness, contrast, saturation and warmth range from -1 to 1.
- Sharpness ranges from 0 to 1; blur is a radius from 0 to 64 pixels.
- Alpha and transparency never change because of an adjustment.
- The preview and the saved result use the same calculation.

## Filters

- **Vivid** raises contrast and saturation.
- **Warm** and **Cool** shift the white balance.
- **B/W** removes color; **Sepia** applies the classic warm tone.
- **Original** returns every adjustment value to zero without changing the crop, rotation or marks.
