# The Details pane

The Details pane shows information about what you select, on the right side of the Explorer.

## Showing and hiding it

Click **Details** at the right end of the command bar, choose **View** > **Details pane**, or press **Alt+Shift+P**. Adperastra remembers your choice.

## What it shows

- **One item:** its name, type, size, location (with a button to copy the path), and the dates it was modified, created, and last opened, plus its attributes, such as Hidden or Read-only.
- **Several items:** how many files and folders are selected and the total size of the files. Folder sizes aren't counted yet.
- **Nothing selected:** the open folder, how many items it has, and its dates.

The first facts appear as soon as you click; the dates and attributes follow a moment later. Adperastra reads them without opening the file, so a file stored online only is never downloaded just to show its details.

Previews and richer information (photo and media details, for example) arrive in a later version.
