# Tab groups

Keep related tabs together without moving their files.

## How to use

- Right-click a tab and choose **Add tab to group > New group**. Give the group a name and color.
- Use its label to collapse or expand it. Right-click the label to rename, change color, ungroup or close the group.
- Drag the label to move the whole group. Removing one tab from a group places it right after the group.

## Good to know

- Ungroup keeps the tabs open. Closing a group checks for unsaved changes.
- Pinned tabs and the permanent Explorer tab stay outside groups.
- Group colors are never the only label: every group also has a name and a tab count.

See also: "Saved groups" and "Working with tabs".
