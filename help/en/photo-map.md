# Photo map

Place indexed photos with location metadata on an optional map.

## How to use

- Enable online map tiles after reviewing the network notice.
- Pan, zoom or choose a photo from the accessible list.

## Good to know

- OpenStreetMap receives tile coordinates for the area viewed; photo files are not uploaded.
- Only visible tiles are fetched and cached according to the provider policy.
- Online-only photos are not downloaded.

See also: "Indexing and privacy".
