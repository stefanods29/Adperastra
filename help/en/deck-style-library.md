# The style library

Choose a manual style for your presentation.

## How to use

Open **Design > Style library**. Choose **Whole presentation** or **Selected slides**. Review a style's colors, fonts, layouts and density, then choose **Apply style**. Review missing-font or overflow warnings before confirming. Use Undo to return to the previous style.

## Good to know

Styles do not write, shorten or delete your text. Manual formatting and unmatched objects are kept. Guidance is only a suggestion. Optional AI generation uses these same styles after a separate review.

See also: "Layouts and themes" and "Adding my own styles".
