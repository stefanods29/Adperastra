# Transitions and titles

## How to use

Choose two adjacent clips and add **Cross-fade**, **Dip to color** or **Wipe**. Review its duration: overlapping clips shortens that track's timeline.

Add a title with a licensed font and choose no animation, fade or slide.

## Good to know

Preview uses the same timing as export. Undo reverses each completed edit.

See also: "Timeline effects and color".
