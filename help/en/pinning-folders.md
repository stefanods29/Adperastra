# Pinning folders

Pin the folders you open often so they stay one click away.

## Pin and unpin

1. Open the folder.
2. Click **Pin folder** at the top right of the folder view.
3. To unpin, open the folder again and click **Unpin folder**, or use the unpin button next to the folder in the left pane.

Pinned folders appear in the left pane under **Pinned** and as cards in Quick access on the Home page.

Your pins and the folders you visited recently are saved in a small file called `explorer-state.json` next to your settings, so they are still there when you restart the app.
