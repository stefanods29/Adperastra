# Installing and removing packs

Add optional tools only when you need them.

## How to use

- Open **Settings** > **App storage**, review a pack's purpose, version, size and license, then choose **Install**.
- Pause or cancel its download from the job card.
- Choose **Update** or **Remove** here later.
- Offline packs are verified in the same way.
- Removing a pack keeps your documents; open jobs or editors must release it first.

Downloads and signatures are checked before tools become available.

See also: "App storage" and "Converting audio and video".
