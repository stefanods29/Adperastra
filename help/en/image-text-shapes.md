# Editable text and shapes

Add a text or shape layer and edit its content or properties in the panel.

## How to use

- Add a text or shape layer and edit its content or properties in the panel.
- Fonts come from the verified licensed inventory.
- Rulers and guides help align content but do not appear in exports.

## Good to know

- Ordinary image export flattens these layers; **Save editable project** keeps them editable.

See also: "Saving image projects".
