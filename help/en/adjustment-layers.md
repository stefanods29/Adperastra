# Adjustment layers and filters

Add an adjustment layer for curves, levels, color, blur, sharpening or noise reduction.

## How to use

- Add an adjustment layer for curves, levels, color, blur, sharpening or noise reduction.
- Change its values, opacity or mask without rewriting the original pixels.
- Reorder it to change which layers it affects.
- Disable it to compare and Undo to reverse a change.

## Good to know

- Export uses the same adjustment math as the preview.

See also: "Image layers".
