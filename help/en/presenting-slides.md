# Presenting

Show slides while keeping notes private. Choose From beginning or From current slide and select the output display. Presenter view shows notes, the next slide and a timer. Use Next and Previous to navigate; Escape ends and returns to the editor. Black screen hides slide content temporarily. If a display disconnects, choose a display before continuing. Notes are never placed on the audience output.

## Good to know

- The presentation opens on the display you choose and confirm. The app never assumes a particular monitor is the second screen.
- The audience window shows slide pixels only; notes, comments and presenter controls stay in the editor.
- The timer counts from the start and excludes paused time. Reset it explicitly.

See also: "Editing presentations" and "Keyboard shortcuts and styles".
