# Accessibility

Combine the options that make the app easier to use.

## How to use

Open Settings > Accessibility and choose Vision, Hearing, Motor and dexterity, Cognitive and learning or Motion and comfort. Windows high contrast and reduced motion are respected automatically.

## Good to know

All main actions have keyboard routes and visible names. Your files are not changed by display preferences.

See also: "Vision accessibility" and "Motor accessibility".
