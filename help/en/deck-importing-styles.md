# Adding my own styles

Reuse safe style information from a template you own.

## How to use

Open **Style library** and choose **Import from template…**. Select a supported template, review the colors, licensed fonts and layouts, and give the style a unique name. Confirm any font mapping, then save it to your library. **Export style…** creates a small style file that can be imported on another installation.

## Good to know

Template text, notes, images, macros and active content are not imported. The template remains unchanged. Imported styles can be removed from your library with Undo available. Built-in styles cannot be removed.

See also: "The style library" and "File compatibility and limits".
