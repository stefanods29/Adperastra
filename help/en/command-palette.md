# Command palette

Find commands, open tabs and places from one search box.

## How to use

Press Ctrl+K and type in your app language or English. Use the arrow keys and Enter to choose a result. Press Esc to return.

## Good to know

Unavailable commands show a reason and cannot run. Opening a location follows the normal file-opening rules. Recent actions stay on this PC.

See also: "Keyboard shortcuts and styles" and "Saved groups".
