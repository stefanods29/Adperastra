# Extracting receipts and invoices

Collect receipt fields in one reviewed table.

## How to use it

Select receipts or invoices, choose **Receipts and invoices to spreadsheet** and review dates, vendor, tax ID, currency, totals and line items beside their sources. Correct uncertain or inconsistent values before export.

## Good to know

Ambiguous dates and currencies are not guessed. Arithmetic warnings do not change totals automatically. This is extraction, not accounting advice. With an API backend, review the displayed scan content and confirm Send once before inference.

See also: "Extracting tables" and "AI privacy".
