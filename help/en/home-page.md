# The Home page

Home is the Explorer tab's starting page.

## Quick access

The cards at the top open your main folders (Desktop, Downloads, Documents, Pictures, Music, Videos) and any folders you have pinned. Click the heading to collapse or expand the grid.

## Recent and Favorites

**Recent** lists files and folders you opened recently: the Windows recent-items list plus folders you visited in Adperastra, newest first. Click a folder to open it.

**Favorites** is empty for now. It will hold items you mark as favorites in a later version; pinned folders live in Quick access instead.

See also: "Pinning folders" and "Browsing folders" in the help list on the left.
