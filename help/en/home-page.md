# The Home page

Home is the Explorer tab's starting page.

## Quick access

The cards at the top open your main folders (Desktop, Downloads, Documents, Pictures, Music, Videos) and any folders you have pinned. Click the heading to collapse or expand the grid.

## Recent and Favorites

**Recent** lists files and folders you opened recently: the Windows recent-items list plus folders you visited in Adperastra, newest first.

**Favorites** lists the files and folders you added with **Add to favorites** in the right-click menu. Right-click a favorite and choose **Remove from favorites** to take it off the list. A favorite on a drive that isn't connected is hidden until the drive is back.

Both lists work like any folder: select items, double-click to open them, or right-click for more, including **Show in folder** to jump to the folder that holds an item. Pinned folders live in Quick access instead.

See also: "Pinning folders", "Browsing folders", and "The right-click menu" in the help list on the left.
