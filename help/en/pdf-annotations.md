# Highlighting and drawing on PDFs

Enable editing, choose Highlight, Draw or Text box and place the mark on a page.

## How to use

Select a mark to change its color, size or text. Undo and Redo change your edit history. Save a copy to keep the original.

## Good to know

Marks are written as standard PDF annotations with appearances. Unsupported structures may prevent saving safely.

See also: PDF comments; Rearranging PDF pages.
