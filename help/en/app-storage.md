# App storage

See the space used by Adperastra and its optional features.

## How to use

- Open **Settings** > **App storage**.
- Review **Packs** and **Stored data**.
- Choose **Clear** beside a category, read what will be lost and confirm.
- Change its limit when available.

## Good to know

- User files are kept.
- Open previews may release their space later.
- Clearing versions or clipboard history loses that stored history.

See also: "Installing and removing packs" and "Background work".
