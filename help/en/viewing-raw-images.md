# Viewing RAW images

View the preview stored in a camera RAW file.

## How to use

- Open a RAW image: CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, DNG, RAF, ORF or RW2.
- The first view uses the preview the camera stored inside the file.
- Choose **Load full resolution** only when you need more detail and a compatible codec is installed.
- Use the zoom and rotate controls to inspect the picture.

## Good to know

- The RAW data is never developed or changed.
- A missing codec may require another app; the viewer explains this and offers **Open in Windows app**.
- Full resolution is never loaded in the background or when a tab is restored.
- Online-only files are not downloaded by the preview.

See also: "Viewing images" and "File details".
