# Tab options

Choose how tabs look and where they open.

## How to use

Open **Settings > Tab options**. Choose width, close-button behavior, new-tab placement and what activates after closing. Hover previews are optional.

## Good to know

The permanent Explorer tab stays open. Previews are read-only and never start playback. Full names remain available to accessibility tools and tooltips.

See also: "Working with tabs".
