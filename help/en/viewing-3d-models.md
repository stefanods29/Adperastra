# Viewing 3D models

Inspect a model from different angles without changing it.

## How to use

- Open a supported model: STL, OBJ, glTF, GLB or 3MF.
- Drag with the mouse to orbit around the model, and use the mouse wheel to zoom.
- With the model canvas focused, use the arrow keys to turn the view and **+** or **-** to zoom.
- Choose **Fit model** to reset the view.
- Choose **Wireframe** to inspect the structure.
- Choose **Measure**, then select two points on the model to see their distance.
- Use **Units** to choose source units, millimeters, centimeters or meters.

## Good to know

- Source units are not always physical units. A model without a declared unit is shown as **Source units**.
- Remote or unsafe resources are not loaded, and texture files outside the model's own folder are refused.
- The model is never changed.
- Some resources of a damaged or unusual model may be missing; the view says so instead of pretending the model is complete.
- Very large or compressed models may need another app.

See also: "Quick preview" and "Opening files".
