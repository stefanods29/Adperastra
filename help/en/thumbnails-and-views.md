# Thumbnails and views

Picture thumbnails help you recognize local files without opening each one.

## How to use

Choose an icon view from **View**. Pictures load as you scroll. Open **Settings > Background work** to pause thumbnail creation.

## Good to know

Online-only files keep their cloud icon. Unsupported or damaged pictures keep their file icon. Your original pictures are never changed.
