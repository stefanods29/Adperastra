# Sorting and filtering

Organize rows without mixing their data.

## How to use

Select the complete rectangle and choose **Sort** or **Filter**. Review whether the first row is a header.

## Good to know

Sorting moves full rows within the chosen range; filtering hides rows that do not match. Clear filter shows them again. Neither action refreshes external data.
