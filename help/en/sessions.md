# Sessions

Choose whether to restore your tabs after restarting.

## How to use sessions

- Open **Settings > Startup** and choose **Restore tabs**.
- Close and reopen the app to restore folder locations and supported file positions.
- Use **Locate…** when a saved file moved.

## Good to know

- Passwords, live access permissions and unsaved document contents are not stored in the session.
- Unsaved recovery is handled separately.
- Missing files do not prevent other tabs from opening.
- Locked folders must be unlocked again.

See also: "Working with tabs" and "Unsaved changes and recovery".
