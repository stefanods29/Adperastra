# Stay in background

Keep a small idle window ready after closing so opening a file from Windows is fast.

## How to use

- Enable **Stay in background** in Startup settings to keep an idle window ready after closing.
- Use the tray icon for **Open**, **Turn off stay in background** and **Quit**.
- **Start with Windows** is a separate choice, and it needs **Stay in background**.

## Good to know

- Unsaved changes are settled before hiding or quitting; **Cancel** keeps the window visible.
- Engines and large caches unload while idle.
- The shown memory figure is measured, not estimated.
- Other background tasks and update checks keep their own switches. Staying in the background never turns them on.
