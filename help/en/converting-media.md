# Converting audio and video

Convert a copy with the optional Media tools pack.

## How to use it

- Select files, choose **Convert to…**, select a compatible format and review quality, time range, destination and metadata.
- Use **Preview sample**, then **Convert**.
- The job card shows progress and Cancel.

## Good to know

- Originals are kept.
- Some codecs depend on the installed build or Windows; unavailable choices stay disabled.
- Stream-copy trimming may be approximate.

See also: "Installing and removing packs" and "Exporting media".
