# Hidden information in Office and media files

Open **Remove hidden information**, inspect the listed fields and select only what you want removed.

## How to use it

- Comments, tracked changes, hidden text and speaker notes change document content, so review their warning separately.
- Removing tracked changes accepts the current visible content.
- Choose **Clean copy** and review the result.

## Good to know

- Unlisted information is not claimed removed.
- Originals remain unchanged.

See also: "Removing hidden information" and "Converting documents".
