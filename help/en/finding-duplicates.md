# Finding duplicates

Review files with verified identical bytes.

## How to use

Choose folders and Find duplicates. Compare the verified groups, select keepers and review any copies proposed for recycling. Nothing is selected for removal automatically.

## Good to know

Similar appearance is not enough. Hard links are one physical file. At least one verified copy must stay. Cleanup is unavailable when the app cannot bind recycling safely to the reviewed objects; opening and reviewing still work.

See also: "Storage view".
