# How Office editing works

Edit supported Office files with Adperastra's own controls and a local editing engine.

## How to use

- Open a supported file and choose **Enable editing** when offered.
- Use the ribbon to edit, then choose **Save** or **Save a copy…**.
- Use **Compatibility and limits** to review what the engine can edit or preserve.

## Good to know

The engine is optional. Files from the internet start in protected view. Macros and external data connections do not run. Some content may be preserved without being editable. If safe preservation is unavailable, use a supported copy or your chosen app.

See also: "File compatibility and limits" and "Unsaved changes and recovery".
