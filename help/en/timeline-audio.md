# Mixing timeline audio

## How to use

Adjust clip and track gain, mute, solo and fades in **Audio mix**. Mute always wins; solo limits playback to the selected eligible tracks or clips. Watch clipping warnings and reduce levels rather than expecting automatic loudness correction.

## Good to know

Add music as an ordinary audio clip. Imported subtitle text stays plain text and can be edited or burned into output.

See also: "Exporting a timeline".
