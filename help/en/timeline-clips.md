# Working with timeline clips

Move clips, trim their ends, **Split** at an interior position or **Slip** the source while keeping the clip's timeline position.

## How to use

**Snap** aligns edges; Alt temporarily disables it. **Ripple delete** removes the chosen interval and closes its gap on affected unlocked tracks; ambiguous or locked edits are refused.

## Good to know

**Undo** reverses a completed gesture.

See also: "Proxies and playback".
