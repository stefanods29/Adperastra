# Turning scans into documents

Turn a scan into a separate editable document.

## How to use it

Select a photo or PDF, choose **Scan to editable document** and review the result beside the original. Select a field to locate its source, correct the text and choose DOCX, Markdown or text export.

## Good to know

OCR and layout can be wrong; review uncertain content. The original remains unchanged. DOCX opens in the viewer until document editing is available. With an API backend, review the displayed scan content and confirm Send once before inference.

See also: "Copying text from images" and "AI quality tiers".
