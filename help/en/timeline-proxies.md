# Proxies and playback

Enable proxy previews to prepare smaller copies for smooth editing. Originals remain unchanged.

## How to use

Proxy jobs show progress and **Cancel**, obey background settings and skip online-only files unless you explicitly open them.

## Good to know

Changed sources invalidate their previews. Playback uses the latest requested position; missing or slow previews do not authorize changing source media.

See also: "The timeline" and "Background work".
