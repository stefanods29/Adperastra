# Installing and updating

Install Adperastra for your Windows account or use the portable version.

## How to use

Download the installer or portable ZIP from the project's release page. Verify its checksum first. Run the installer, or unpack the portable ZIP into a writable folder. Updates are optional; a check opens the release page and never installs anything automatically.

## Good to know

This release is unsigned. Windows may show ‘Windows protected your PC’. Verify the download before choosing ‘More info’ and ‘Run anyway’. Upgrades keep settings and files. During uninstall, Keep my settings and data is the default.

See also: "Verifying a download" and "Portable mode".
