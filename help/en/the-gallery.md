# The Gallery

See pictures from folders you choose in one place.

## How to use

- Open **Gallery** in the navigation pane.
- Choose **Add picture folder…** and pick a folder.
- Keep **Include subfolders** on to include its folders.
- Choose **Date taken** or **Date modified** to group pictures.
- Open a picture with Enter or a double-click. Press Space for a quick preview.

## Good to know

- Removing a folder from Gallery does not delete it or its pictures.
- Online-only pictures keep a cloud icon and are not downloaded in the background.
- Folders you did not choose are not scanned.
- Some details may be missing.

See also: "Quick preview" and "Background work".
