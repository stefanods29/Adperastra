# Image selections

Choose a rectangle, ellipse, lasso, polygon, magic wand or color selection. Replace, add, subtract or intersect selections; use **Invert** or **Feather** when needed. The wand follows connected similar pixels; **Select by color** finds similar pixels across the image. **Copy** captures the chosen layer or composite; **Paste** makes a new layer. **Cut** changes pixels only after copying succeeds.

See also: Transforming image content.
