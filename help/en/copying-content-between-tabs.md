# Copying content between tabs

Reuse supported text, cells, images and pages in another editor.

## How to use it

- Copy or drag a selection, activate the destination and paste or drop it.
- Choose an insertion if asked.
- Review any formatting changes.

## Good to know

- Copying captures the selected revision and does not change the source.
- Protected views cannot accept edits.
- Content from locked folders needs an explicit decision before an unencrypted copy can leave the folder.

See also: "Dragging between tabs" and "Locked folders".
