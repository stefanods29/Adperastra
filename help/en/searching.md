# Searching

Find files in folders you chose to index.

## How to use

- Choose indexed folders in **Settings > Indexing**.
- Enter a query and open a result normally.
- **Save search** stores the query for later.

## Good to know

- Coverage may be incomplete while indexing.
- Short name queries match prefixes.
- Online-only content is not downloaded.

See also: "Search modes and prefixes".
