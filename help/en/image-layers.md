# Image layers

Choose **Advanced** to combine images as layers. Add or duplicate a layer, change order, visibility, lock, opacity or blend mode in **Layers**. Masks hide pixels without deleting them. **Merge down** combines two layers; **Flatten** combines the image after confirmation. Undo restores supported changes within the history limit. Ordinary image export is flattened; editable projects are separate.

See also: Image selections.
