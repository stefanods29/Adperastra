## Goal and exclusions

Choose tab behavior, date/size/name display, opening/confirmation preferences, isolated local profiles and complete portable data storage. Existing per-extension default apps and09a Startup remain the authorities. No remote profile sync, multiple independent app windows, account system, new terminal, automatic cleanup or AI.
