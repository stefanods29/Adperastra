## Goal and exclusions

Edit supported slide decks with thumbnails, notes, masters/layouts/themes, text/images/shapes/tables/charts, supported effects and a local presentation mode. Preserve imported unsupported effects during save. No AI slide generation, image generation, writing suggestions, model packs, online assets, remote presenting or scripting/macros. Manual eight-style library belongs to22b. Full v1.0 acceptance also requires22b and the other included AI steps; this guide alone does not claim milestone completion.
