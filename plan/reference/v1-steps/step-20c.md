# Step 20c: AI for documents (★★★★)

**Needs:** 20b, 17a. **Also read:** `plan/ai.md` (especially the quality rules: small local models are limited, so tune carefully).

## Tasks
- Select text and choose **Rewrite, Shorten, Expand, Simplify, Fix grammar, Change tone** (formal, friendly, persuasive, neutral), or **Translate**; **Write alt text** for images; **Draft a section about...** and **Continue writing**.
- Results always appear as a suggestion beside the text (accept, retry, edit, or discard) and are inserted as tracked changes if track changes is on; never replace text silently. Keep the paragraph's formatting and styles.
- Small, specific jobs only (one paragraph or section at a time), structured output, prompts with examples in each language, and an evaluation set for each action with scores in the step report; weak actions are marked Experimental or left out.
- Offer the quality tiers from `plan/ai.md` and show which one is active.

AI writing help in documents; quality tiers and what to expect.

Each action works in English and Spanish on my sample documents, passes its evaluation set, and never changes text without my approval.
